Client logos go in this folder.

1. Save each logo as a PNG with a transparent background, around 400px wide.
2. Name it simply, for example: acme-industries.png
3. Open index.html, find the CLIENT LOGOS comment block, and replace
   <div class="logo-slot">Client Logo</div>
   with
   <div class="logo-slot"><img src="assets/img/clients/acme-industries.png" alt="Acme Industries"></div>

Logos display in grey and turn full colour on hover. Delete any slots you do not fill.
